Do your eyes water while watching TV? 📺💧
Sounds opposite, but it's true — watery eyes often mean your eyes are TOO DRY.
When your tear film is unstable from dryness, your eyes overreact and produce reflex tears. That's watery eyes = dry eye disease.
Signs to check:
• Watering while watching TV / screen
• Burning or gritty feeling
• Blurred vision that clears after blinking
It's not normal. Get your tear film checked.
