Is it normal to see floaters? 👁️

Looking at a bright sky or white wall and seeing tiny dots, squiggly worms, or cobwebs drifting? That's what floaters look like — POV view above.

✓ NORMAL: Few tiny dots, drifts slowly, stable for months
⚠️ WHEN TO WORRY: Sudden shower of floaters / Flashes of light / Dark curtain or shadow

Few and stable = usually okay. Sudden shower = get checked same day.
