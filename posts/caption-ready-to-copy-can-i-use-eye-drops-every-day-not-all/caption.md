"Can I use eye drops every day?" 💧
Not all EYE DROPS are safe for daily use! Some can make dry eyes worse!
✅ SAFE for daily (often):
• Preservative-free artificial tears
• As per doctor's dosage
⚠️ NOT for daily:
• Redness/whitening drops
• Steroid/antibiotic — Rx only
• Overuse can worsen dryness
Daily? Check type, expiry, preservatives. Ask your doctor.
