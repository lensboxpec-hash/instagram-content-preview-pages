"Eating carrots will fix my vision" 🥕👀 — My favourite myth!

Truth: Carrots are rich in Vitamin A and great for MAINTAINING eye health. But they can't FIX an existing power, cataract, or dry eye.

It's like putting premium fuel in a car with a flat tire — good fuel doesn't fix the tire.

🥕 HELPS MAINTAIN — Vitamin A supports eye health
👁️‍🗨️ FIXES THE PROBLEM — Eye check finds and corrects issues
