"Why do my eyes burn when I wake up?" 😣🌅
Burning or stinging after sleep is common — eyes can dry out overnight.
👁️ Common: Dry Eye
• Tears dry overnight
• Fan/AC on face
• Less blinking in sleep
• Late screen time
⚠️ Watch for: Infection
• Sticky watery discharge
• Redness & swelling
• Crusty lids in morning
If it happens regularly, get it checked!
