"Why do my eyes feel tired all the time?" 😴👁️

Just like your body needs rest after work, your eyes need breaks too — especially with screens!

Try the simple 20-20-20 rule:

👉 Every 20 mins — Look away
👉 Look 20 feet away — Distance view
👉 For 20 seconds — Rest eyes

A simple habit to reduce tired eyes & headaches. Your eyes will thank you!
