How often should I get my eyes checked? 👁️📅
It's not one-size-fits-all — here's a simple guide:
• UNDER 40: Healthy eyes — Once a year | Glasses/contacts / 6hr+ screen — Once a year
• 40-60: Presbyopia starts — Every 12 months | Over 60 — Every 6-12 months
• HIGH RISK: Diabetes / BP / Family h/o Glaucoma — Every 6 months | Sudden blur / pain / flashes — Check immediately
Once a year if you're under 40, more often if you have diabetes or family history!
Not one-size-fits-all — mark your calendar. 🗓️
