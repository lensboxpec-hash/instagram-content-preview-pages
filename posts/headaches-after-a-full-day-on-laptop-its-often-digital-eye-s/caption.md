Headaches after a full day on laptop? 💻🤦‍♀️
It's often digital eye strain — your eye muscles working too hard from screen focus, less blinking, and close distance.
Try this today:
👁️ 20-20-20 Rule — Every 20 mins, look 20 feet away for 20 sec
💧 Blink Often — Screens cut blink rate by half
📏 Arm's Length — Keep screen at arm's distance, slightly below eye level
Small habits = big relief. If headaches persist, get your eyes checked.
