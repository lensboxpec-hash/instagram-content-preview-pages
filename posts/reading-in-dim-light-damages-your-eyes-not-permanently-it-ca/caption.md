Reading in dim light damages your eyes? 📖💡 — Not permanently!

It CAN make your eyes feel tired, strain, and you may blink less. But it won't cause lasting damage.

Better to:
💡 Use good warm light
👀 Take breaks every 20 mins
💧 Blink often

Your eyes will thank you.
